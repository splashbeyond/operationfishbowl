# TimeTank — App Store Overview

**Title:** TimeTank: Screen Time & Focus
**Subtitle:** Meet Finn. Keep his tank clean.
**Category:** Productivity
**Platform:** iOS (Native)

---

## App Store Description

Are you tired of screen time apps that just lock you out of your phone, yell at you with guilt trips, or trap you in hidden, expensive subscriptions?

**TimeTank** is a screen time app designed for anyone who wants to break the mindless scrolling cycle and build a healthier relationship with their phone. Instead of putting up a harsh wall that makes you want to delete the app altogether, TimeTank uses gentle, smart boundaries — visualized through a living digital fish tank and your new companion, **Finn**.

When you stay intentional with your phone, Finn thrives. The water stays bright and clear. When you slide past your limits, the water gets murkier — and Finn feels it. It's a simple, emotionally engaging, and deeply personal way to take back control of your day.

---

## Why TimeTank Works

- **Meet Finn:** Your fish lives in your tank. He swims freely when you're focused and slows down when the water gets murky. Finn makes your screen time feel real — because nobody wants to hurt their fish.

- **Flexible Daily Budgets:** Other apps force you into rigid schedules that don't fit real life. TimeTank gives you a daily time allowance for your favorite distraction apps to use whenever you want. You choose the budget; we help you stick to it.

- **Smart Whitelisting:** Stop getting penalized for using your phone productively. TimeTank lets you entirely exempt essential utilities like maps, banking apps, or music players. Finn's tank only reacts to the specific scrolling apps you choose to track.

- **The "Speed Bump" Intercept:** Instead of locking you out entirely, TimeTank interrupts your automatic reflexes. The exact moment you unconsciously tap a distracting app, a calm, mindful pause screen appears — with Finn watching — giving you a second to ask yourself, _"Do I really need to open this right now?"_

- **Earn Currents:** Stay within your budget and earn Currents at midnight. Spend them in the marketplace on new tank decorations, Finn styles, and accessories. Your consistency builds something beautiful.

---

## How It Works

1. **Pick Your Apps** — Choose the specific apps you want to cut back on.
2. **Set Your Budget** — Give yourself a daily time allowance for those apps.
3. **Watch the Tank** — Keep your phone down to keep the water crystal clear. If you scroll past your daily limit, the water gradually murkies up.
4. **Grow Your Tank** — Earn Currents daily and build a personalized sanctuary for Finn over time.
