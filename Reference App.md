## 1. Brainrot: Screen Time Control (by Smolworks Inc.)

This is the most popular productivity app under this name. It gamifies reducing screen time by showing a cute brain avatar that "decays" or deteriorates the more you doomscroll.

**Key Features:**

- **Visual Gamification:** A brain avatar that visually decays as you use blocked apps, providing a humorous but effective guilt trip.
    
- **App & Website Blocking:** Instant blocking capabilities for your most distracting apps and sites.
    
- **Smart Scheduling:** Set up automatic blocking schedules for bedtime, mornings, or work meetings.
    
- **Focus Timer:** A built-in Pomodoro-style timer for deep work.
    
- **Mindful Breaks:** Allows you to take intentional breaks without feeling guilty.
    
- **Privacy-Focused:** Uses Apple's Screen Time API, meaning all tracking happens locally on your device.

## 2. Brainrot: Focus & Screen Time (by Gal Linial)

Another productivity app, but this one focuses heavily on a percentage-based "Brain Score" rather than an avatar.

**Key Features:**

- **Brain Score:** You start every day at 100%. The more you exceed your set screen time goal, the lower your score drops (shifting from Green to Orange to Red).
    
- **Home Screen Widget:** Keeps your current score visible every time you unlock your phone.
    
- **Mind Check:** Tracks granular data most apps miss: how many times you picked up your phone, your first pickup time, notification counts, and your longest unbroken scrolling session.
    
- **Score History:** A color-coded monthly calendar to track your performance over time.
    
- **Smart Notifications:** Nudges you when your score hits milestones like 75%, 50%, 25%, and 10%, plus morning and evening reports.
    

## 3. Brainrot Stories (by Paolo Alvarez)

This is an entertainment app rather than a productivity tool. It uses AI to generate stories based on popular internet culture and "brainrot" memes.

**Key Features:**

- **Instant AI Story Generation:** Type a prompt and the AI generates a story featuring characters like Skibidi Toilet, Camera Man, etc.
    
- **Photo-to-Story:** Snap a picture of your room or a toy, and the AI will weave it into a narrative.
    
- **Narrator Styles:** Choose from different tones, including "Brainrot Absurd" for chaotic internet humor.
    
- **Text-to-Speech:** Listen to the generated stories narrated aloud.
    

## 4. "Brainrot" Games

There are also several hyper-casual puzzle and trivia games capitalizing on the meme:

- **Find All Brainrot (by Mounia Bouhssini):** A trivia/catch game where you collect popular internet meme characters.
    
- **Catch & Steal Tricky Brainrot (by Hira Arshad):** A puzzle game featuring tricky riddles, logic tests, and out-of-the-box thinking challenges themed around internet humor.
